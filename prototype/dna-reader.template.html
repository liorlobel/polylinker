Everything runs in this page. There is no server, no account and no upload — open it from a USB stick on a locked-down PC and it still works. Drag a file anywhere onto the window, or use Open file….

Reads the block-framed SnapGene .dna container (sequence, topology and methylation flags, features, primers, notes, and the xz-compressed history tree — detected but not yet inflated), plus GenBank and FASTA. Format is sniffed from content, not from the file extension. Restriction sites are computed live from a fifty-enzyme textbook set, with circular wraparound handled; these agree with Biopython on 5,587 sites across 33 real plasmids.

Save GenBank is the way out of .dna: plain text, read by ApE, UGENE, Benchling, Biopython and SnapGene itself, with feature colours written in the ApE convention. Map as SVG gives you a vector figure you can finish in Illustrator or Inkscape. Test .dna writes a .dna from scratch, deliberately omitting the two derived cache blocks — open it in SnapGene to find out whether they are optional.
